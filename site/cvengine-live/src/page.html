Questions, answered

Do I need an AI model?

No. cvENGINE discovers, scans, matches and validates without one. AI is off until you switch it on, with your own key for Anthropic, OpenAI or OpenRouter, or a local model you run.

What will you scan?

Only domains and addresses you prove you own, from your own Sentinel. The checks are read-only: no exploit payloads, no password lists, nothing that changes a host.

Can AI close a finding on its own?

It reviews the evidence and ranks findings, and the server checks every answer. It never closes a finding that a check proved.

Can you scan my internal networks?

Yes, with a Sentinel you run inside that network. It connects out to your cvENGINE, so nothing on your network has to accept an inbound connection.

Is my data mixed with other customers'?

No. Each customer has a separate cvENGINE server with its own database, and a separate Sentinel.

What if I have more than 3,000 addresses?

Choose "More than 3,000" when you request early access and we will size a plan with you.
