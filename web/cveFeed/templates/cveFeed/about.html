Built on reNgine

CVEngine is a fork of reNgine 2.x by Yogesh Ojha, and still uses its code. Thanks to everyone who built it, including Aju100, d1pakda5, subha7595, Suprita-25, TheBinitGhimire, Vinay Leo and Erdem Ozgen. The full list is on reNgine's contributors page.
