How cvENGINE works

Add a domain name. cvENGINE discovers the rest, then proves what is exploitable.

Most scanners hand you a list of everything that might be wrong. cvENGINE hands you the short list of what is exposed, exploitable and worth fixing first, with the evidence attached.

  1. 1

    Discover everything you own

    From one domain, cvENGINE finds the subdomains, addresses, certificates and web apps behind it, including the ones nobody remembered to list. New hosts stream into your inventory while the scan is still running.

    bbotcrt.shgowitness

  2. 2

    Map every exposed service

    Every open port, not a guess at the popular ones. Each service is fingerprinted for its product, version and operating system, and web hosts are read for the technologies and libraries they run. Hosts behind a firewall or CDN are recognized, so the edge is never mistaken for the application.

    naabunmapnucleiwafw00fWPScan

  3. 3

    Match against the world's CVEs, only when it fits

    New vulnerabilities arrive from eight public feeds the moment they publish and are rated fire, smoke or ice by how dangerous they are right now. A CVE becomes a match only when the host runs that exact product at an affected version. No product-name guessing, no noise.

    CISA KEVEPSSNVDEUVDOSVExploit-DB

  4. 4

    Prove it, safely

    The AI reviews each match and, when no test exists, writes a nuclei template on the spot. cvENGINE then reproduces the finding with read-only checks and records the exact command and result. The AI can rule a match out, but it can never confirm one by itself: only hard evidence does.

    AI triagenucleiversion probes

  5. 5

    Fix the right thing first

    Every host gets a 0 to 100 score and a temperature. The dashboard shows what changed since you last looked, how long the worst item has been open, and the hygiene problems nobody scans for: expiring certificates, missing SPF, DKIM and DMARC, weak TLS. Triage findings and track each fix in one place.

    host scorechange trackinghygiene

Why you can trust a finding

  • Evidence, not opinion. Every finding lists the checks that ran and what they returned.
  • Safe by design. Checks are read-only. No exploit payloads, no password lists, and nothing that changes a host.
  • Always watching. Scans repeat on a schedule, certificates are watched daily, and every change to your attack surface is recorded with screenshots.
  • Yours to inspect. Open source under the GPL-3.0, built on proven scanners, and self-hosted so your inventory stays with you.
