Where your data goes

cvENGINE sends AI jobs to the one provider an App Admin chooses in Settings: Anthropic, OpenAI or OpenRouter (hosted services: the data leaves your network), or Ollama, vLLM or LM Studio (a model server you run: the data goes only where that server is). The requests are made by the AI reviewer container, never by your browser.

cvENGINE has no agreement with any provider about what they keep or whether they train on it. Read your provider's data-retention and training terms, and use an account or a plan whose terms you accept, or a model you host yourself.

The provider's API key is stored encrypted, is never shown again after it is saved, and is sent only to that provider's address.
